Games Widget – Privacy Policy
Last Updated: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction
Games Widget is a Windows desktop application designed to help users organize, manage, and launch their installed PC games from one convenient interface.

This Privacy Policy explains how the application accesses, uses, and stores information.
2. Information We Access
Games Widget may access the following information on your computer:
- Installed game names and locations.
- Game launcher information (Steam, Epic Games, GOG).
- Game executable paths.
- Game artwork and wallpapers.
- Application settings and custom game groups.
This information is accessed to provide the application's game detection, organization, and launching functionality.
3. Local Data Storage
Games Widget stores game library information, application settings, and user preferences locally on your computer.

The application does not require account registration or a personal user profile.

Your game library is not automatically uploaded to a Games Widget server.
4. Third-Party Services
Games Widget may communicate with external services to retrieve game information and artwork.

These services may include Steam, Epic Games Store, and other artwork providers.

Certain information, such as game titles or search identifiers, may be transmitted to these services when requesting artwork.

Third-party services operate independently and are subject to their own privacy policies.
5. Internet Connectivity
An internet connection is required for certain features, including retrieving online game artwork.

Core game organization and library management functionality is designed to operate locally.
6. Advertising
If third-party advertisements are introduced, advertising providers may collect certain technical information, such as IP addresses, device information, and advertising identifiers.

Additional information about advertising partners, tracking technologies, and available privacy controls will be provided when applicable.
7. Data Protection
We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with Games Widget.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.
8. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated as Games Widget evolves, particularly when new features, integrations, or advertising services are introduced.

The latest version will be available through the project's official GitHub repository.
