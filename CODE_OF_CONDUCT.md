# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone participating in the Games Widget project.

We welcome contributions from people of all experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

All contributors and community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate of others.
- Communicate professionally and constructively.
- Accept feedback and differing opinions.
- Focus on improving the project.
- Help create a positive environment for everyone.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting or disrespectful comments.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any behavior that makes the community unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for reviewing reported violations and taking appropriate action.

Depending on the situation, this may include warnings, removal of comments, or restrictions on participation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and other project-related interactions.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository maintainer privately through GitHub.

All reports will be reviewed respectfully and confidentially.
